# Calendar — what moves the numbers

Check this before calling any trend. Dates are approximate; update each school year.

| period | effect on edu TikTok | content implication |
|---|---|---|
| Sep – mid Oct | new school year, interest in "ôn lại lớp dưới" and new-grade basics | foundations, F-02 |
| Nov, Mar | mid-term exams (giữa kỳ) | exam-style F-05, F-01 on tested chapters |
| Dec – mid Jan | end-of-term exams (cuối kỳ I) | review series |
| Tết (late Jan – Feb, ~2 weeks) | study interest drops sharply | don't judge experiments in this window |
| Apr – May | cuối kỳ II + THPT final push | F-05, lớp 12 heavy |
| Late June | **THPT national exam** | peak demand for lớp 12 the 6 weeks before |
| Jul – Aug | summer; lớp 10 entrance exams early June | lighter, preview next grade |

Rule: never compare a post from a peak week with one from a trough week as if they
were equal; say which window each readout fell in.
