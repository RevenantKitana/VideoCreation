# Brand — look and voice

**Look** (enforced by the engine; don't fight it): flat editorial — cream `#F5EFE2`,
jade `#1F6F5C`, ink `#1C1C1A`; clay `#B4553A` only for "this is wrong". No gradients,
no stock footage, no emoji on screen. Font Be Vietnam Pro. Wordmark "AIDUCATION · <MÔN>".

**Voice**: calm teacher, not hype. Short sentences. Say what the student should do.
Default AI voice Minh Quân Pro; Trúc Ly for a female voice.

**Caption template** (`caption.txt`): one line hook as a question or promise + 3–5
hashtags: one subject tag, one grade/exam tag, one topic tag. e.g.
`Xét dấu đạo hàm trong 1 phút ✅ #toan12 #onthithpt #daoham #hocvoiaiducation`

**Never**: claim exam predictions, name competitors, make up statistics ("90% học sinh…"
only if we have the data), show student names or faces.
