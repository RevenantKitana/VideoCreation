# Playbook — weekly review

Once a week (or when asked "tuần này thế nào").

1. `./os scorecard --at 48h` and `./os scorecard --at 7d`.
2. Read this week's `days/*/review.md` and the plan's experiment queue.
3. Write `days/<today>/weekly.md`:
   - Progress vs the goal (number now vs target, on pace or not).
   - Each experiment that has enough posts: verdict (won / lost / inconclusive) citing
     readouts. Inconclusive is a valid answer.
   - Patterns that deserve a finding (`./os finding …`).
   - Proposed plan amendment (if any) — ask the person before appending it.
4. Tell the person the 3 most important lines, in Vietnamese.
