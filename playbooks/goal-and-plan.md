# Playbook — set the goal, write the plan

## Goal intake (5 questions max, conversational, in Vietnamese)
1. What result, by when? (e.g. "5.000 follower trong 60 ngày", "200 lượt lưu/tuần",
   "30 lượt tải app/tuần từ TikTok"). Push for a number and a date.
2. Which account (handle), and is it a personal or Business account?
3. Audience: which grades/exams/subjects (e.g. lớp 12 ôn thi THPT môn Toán)?
4. Capacity: how many videos per day/week can they realistically approve and post?
   Who records (AI voice / own voice / cloned)?
5. Anything off-limits (topics, competitors, style)?

Write `goals/G-00N.md` from `goals/TEMPLATE.md`. Only one goal is `active`; mark the old
one `closed` with a one-line outcome.

## The plan: `plans/G-00N.plan.md`
Sections:
- **Target math** — what the goal implies per week (e.g. 5.000 follows / 8 weeks / 10
  posts a week ≈ 60 follows per post, vs our current T-07; say if that's realistic).
- **Phases** — Weeks 1–2 *explore*: test 3–4 formats × 3 hooks at 1–2 posts/day, one
  variable at a time. Weeks 3–6 *exploit*: 70/20/10 mix around the winners. Last weeks
  *scale*: series + posting cadence.
- **Series** — 1–3 recurring formats from `context/formats.md` with a numbering scheme
  (e.g. "Dạng bài #12").
- **Experiments queue** — E-001…: hypothesis, the one variable, variants, what result
  would decide it, readout used (usually 48h).
- **Calendar** — exam dates / holidays from `context/calendar.md` that shift topics.
- **Daily loop** — reference daily-review.md.

Show the person a 6–8 line summary and get ok.

**Changing the plan later:** append `## Amendment YYYY-MM-DD` with what changed and which
finding/review caused it. Never delete earlier text.
